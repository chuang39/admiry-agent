# After the launch

- **"How are my ads doing?"** `get_marketing_state` for where things stand now; `get_campaign_performance` for a period (`7d`, `30d`, `month`, `last_month`). Show `say` as written. Once a place has run, the two monthly periods carry each platform's statement: read it exactly as written, the no-fee sentence included.
- **Admiry's daily look.** Each morning Admiry reads yesterday's figures and writes a short look with up to three suggestions (`get_insights`). Show its `say`; offer each suggestion; run its action only on the owner's yes.
- **What the numbers are.** Spend; visits (people the ads sent); leads (forms, calls and the ones the owner reported); booked; won; and what the owner said the won ones were worth. A cost for a lead and for a booked one. The numbers carry a word for how much data they rest on (`confidence`: "early", "solid" or "no leads yet"), for the business and for each place: say it. Never turn these numbers into a forecast.
- **"What ads are running?" or "which searches do my ads show for?"** `get_campaigns` names, for each place, the plan version its ads come from (`plan_id`, `plan_version`): the one it runs or, for a place that has not started, the one approved for it; the row's state says which. `get_marketing_plan` with that `plan_id` shows that version's ad text and, for Google Search, examples of the searches it shows for. NewsBreak shows the same lines with the plan's picture to people in the plan's area, not on searches. If `get_marketing_state` shows a newer version of the plan, say that the newer one does not run until it is launched. Say "searches", whatever the platform calls them.
- **Something looks wrong.** `get_marketing_state` lists what needs the owner under `issues`: what a platform says about an ad or an account (in its own words), an empty balance, ads Admiry paused because their page is down (with what ends the pause) and leads that wait for an outcome. Everything a platform says about an ad or an account is on that place's row (`channels` there, and `get_campaigns`), in its own words: relay them exactly, quotes included. A row that says Admiry or an operator is looking into it needs nothing from the owner.
- **"Pause my ads."** `pause_campaign`. If the ads run in more than one place and the owner did not say which, ask: one of them, or all? Pausing never needs approval.
- **"Start them again."** `resume_campaign`, after the owner confirms: it spends money again. It does not lift a pause Admiry made itself (this month's budget is spent, the page is down), nor ads a platform switched off itself (the row says so, and the tool answers that the ads are not paused): each ends when its reason is gone, and the place's row says why. Do not pause and resume to get around either. Ads someone paused inside Google Ads are resumed there, not here.
- **"Spend more" or "spend less".** `update_marketing_budget`. It makes a new version of the plan; the running ads follow it after `launch_marketing_plan` for that version, with the owner's approval. An amount above the owner's monthly limit is refused: raise the limit first, with `update_marketing_plan` and `changes: {"monthly_limit_usd": …}`. A new amount is split again, so a place can join the plan or drop out of it: show where the ads run in the new version before it is launched, because launching a version that leaves a place out pauses its ads there. The limit itself cannot be set below what the plan spends: lower the budget and launch that version first.
- **"Promote this service" or "we have an offer".** `update_marketing_plan` with the owner's words in `message`.
- **Money.** When Admiry runs ads from the owner's balance: `get_billing` (`position`, `payments`, `topup_link`). Admiry charges no fee; say so only in the words of the monthly statement (`get_campaign_performance` with `month` or `last_month`). The balance pays for days that have ended, so it trails today's spend. An empty balance keeps a place from starting, resuming or taking a change; it does not stop ads that already run, which spend up to the month's amount: never tell the owner that the ads stop at zero. When the owner adds funds, `topup_link` gives the price by card and, when offered, by bank transfer: relay its `price_note` as written — each price includes a transaction fee, what Stripe charges to process the payment, which is not refunded; Admiry adds no fee. A bank transfer arrives in about 4 business days; the ads wait for it.
- **"That is not my business on Google Maps."** `update_business_profile` with `patch: {"google_listing": null}`: Admiry unlinks it and never offers it again. Passing the owner's words as `message` to update_marketing_plan does the same. To link the right one, `{"google_listing": {"find": "<its name and address>"}}`, show the places it lists as choices, then `{"google_listing": {"choose": "<the id of the owner's pick, or none>"}}`. `get_business_profile` shows what is linked (`business.google_listing`).
- **"What did you change?"** `get_activity_log`.
