# When something goes wrong

- **A tool says it was replaced.** Admiry's tools changed since you saved this guide. Read the connector docs and this guide again, then use the tool it names.
- **401.** The owner has to sign in again: follow the server's OAuth sign-in. Never ask the owner for a key.
- **"lacks the 'manage' scope" (or 'leads').** The owner connected you with fewer permissions. They can connect again and allow more; until then, without 'manage' you can read and not change, and without 'leads' you can neither read nor record leads.
- **"a setup key cannot…".** The owner has not signed in yet: `connect_account` returns their sign-in link.
- **The approval code is refused.** Ask the owner to read it again from their approval page. After five wrong codes in an hour the approval locks for up to an hour. Never try codes.
- **"this plan is already approved".** The owner approved on their page: nothing is left to confirm. `get_campaigns` shows where each place stands.
- **"this business has expired".** The 24-hour draft this access belonged to lapsed, or was folded into the business the owner signed in for, and the access with it: the owner connects you again through their sign-in.
- **"rate limit … retry_after=N".** Wait N seconds.
- **No answer came back** (a timeout, a dropped connection). For a tool that takes an `idempotency_key`, send the same call again right away, unchanged, with the same key: if the first one went through, Admiry answers with its first answer and does nothing twice. That holds for a day, and only while the first answer is still true: a pause that was lifted since, or a plan version that was replaced, is not answered again; the key is refused, and a new key does it. `create_marketing_plan` takes no key and starts over: look at `get_marketing_state` before sending it again. `update_landing_page` with `write`, `revise`, `edit` or `preview_html` makes a new preview each time, and a lost preview cannot be found again: send the call again and a new preview is made (`write` and `revise` spend one of the day's model calls). `update_landing_page` with `action: "build_site"` or `"fix_headline"` makes a new preview on every call that builds, and none on one that only asks a question; it spends one of the day's model calls only when the facts its words come from changed. `get_landing_page` with `view: "site"` shows the preview already made. Every new request gets a new key, never one used before; "idempotency_key already used for a different request" means the key was used before, for another call or for this one before things changed: send a new key.
- **A platform refuses an ad or an account** (`get_campaigns` shows it, and `get_marketing_state` lists a refused ad under `issues`; before a launch, `get_connected_accounts` shows a refused Google Ads account): relay the platform's words exactly, and do not suggest a way around its policy. NewsBreak's account is Admiry's to open: its row says when Admiry is on it.
- **A place's ads could not be launched** (its row in `get_campaigns` says so). It does not try again by itself: do what the sentence asks, then make a new version of the plan with `update_marketing_plan` and launch it with the owner's approval.
- **The owner wants a person, or is unhappy with Admiry.** `contact_support` with their words (`message`), a one-sentence `summary`, the `reason` (human, help or dissatisfied) and, when you have them, the last few turns (`context`). Show `say`: it tells them what happens next. If it answers that this is not available yet, say exactly that and promise no reply.
- **Admiry cannot answer a question** (`ask_admiry` says so): do not fill the gap yourself. If `next` offers a person, offer `contact_support`.

## Before a launch: the checklist

`get_marketing_state`'s `setup.checklist` lists everything that stops the latest plan from launching, in order, and `ready` says when nothing does. While `ready` is false, fix the items in order: for each item with a `fix`, call `fix.tool` with `fix.args`. Where `fix.ask_owner` is present, ask the owner that question first and send their answer in place of the `<…>` placeholder -- never invent a value the owner did not give. Where `fix.choices` is present, offer the choices as written and send the one the owner picks. Read the state again after each fix: the list is checked afresh on Admiry's side, and one fix may clear another item. An item with `fix: null`: relay its problem, and its suggestion when it has one. Money and the account are not items: `setup.steps` has them. The rules themselves live with Admiry, so an item is never something to argue with: fix it, or relay it.

The most common kinds, one line each:

- **The ad text.** Admiry writes the headlines and descriptions again: `update_marketing_plan` with the `message` the fix carries; nothing to ask.
- **A phone or an address.** Only the owner knows it: ask `fix.ask_owner`, then `update_business_profile` with the answer where the placeholder was.
- **The page.** Once the page is changed, `get_landing_page` with `view: "check"` runs the check again; a page not there yet is the owner's choice between the ways `fix.choices` offers (Admiry builds it, you design it and Admiry hosts it, or the website they already have): ask `fix.ask_owner` and send the one they pick.
- **A budget.** The owner picks one of the choices; `update_marketing_budget` with its arguments.
- **A note Admiry left after its review.** Admiry looked at the ads before they went live and left a note at the top of the list: do what it says (its fix, when it has one), then confirm the plan again.
