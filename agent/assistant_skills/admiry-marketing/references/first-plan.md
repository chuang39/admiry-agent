# A first plan and its launch

1. `get_marketing_state`. If a consultation is already open, `next` is its question: continue at step 3. If a plan exists, show it with `get_marketing_plan` and go on at step 5.
2. With nothing begun, `create_marketing_plan` with the owner's words in `message` and everything you already know: `website_url`, `goal`, `service`, `location`, `budget`, `preferred_outcome`, `known_facts`. If the owner's message carries a session code from Admiry's website ("continue session hs_…"), pass it as `handoff` and nothing else: the consultation resumes where the website left it. For a business that already has a consultation or a plan, this tool starts over and reads the business again from the new words: use it then only when the owner asks to start again.
3. Admiry says what it understood and asks its questions. Unless Admiry already knows the business's website, its very first question asks for one: paste the address to have Admiry read it (one crawl, one reading, exactly as `website_url` does), or send "No website". Once a site is read, the answer carries `reading` (the name, services, area, phone and platform Admiry took from it) and `say` names them, so you can show them for the owner to correct. An address that cannot be read is asked again once, with Try again and Ask me instead as its options (send `answers` with `{"website": {"choice": "retry"}}` or `"ask_instead"`, or a corrected address as `message`); a second failure goes on without the site. Without a website, Admiry asks the business's name -- it looks the name up on Google Maps, and a close place found there is asked about right after it, with the listing question below -- and then roughly what the business offers: a few words are enough, and they join the business's services and the lead-service choices. For each question: it is the owner's to answer. Show `say`, put the question to them with its options, and send exactly their answer with `update_marketing_plan`: `answers` when the owner picked an option (`{"objective": {"choice": "bookings"}}`), `message` when they typed. Never pick or infer an answer yourself; if the owner already said it in their own words earlier, confirm it with them in one line before you send it. To correct an earlier answer before the plan is built, send `answers` with that question's id again. When a question asks which Google Maps place is the business, show each place as a choice with "None of these" and send the owner's pick; never pick one for them. A place the owner picks becomes the business's listing and, unless they gave an address already, where the ads centre. Admiry asks where the ads should centre when nothing yet puts the business on the map (no place picked, no address given): send the owner's address, or their city and ZIP code, as `message`; Admiry answers "Centred on …". An owner who would rather say later says so, and gives it later with `update_business_profile` (`locations` with `address`).
4. When the answer says the plan can be built, call `get_marketing_plan`. It can take half a minute or more. Show every card and the footnote: the goal, where the ads run and why, the budget and its levels, what the budget should do in 30 days with each assumption and its basis, and the ad as it will read. When the ad's card (`ads_preview`) carries an `image_note`, relay it, with or without an `image`: it says which picture NewsBreak shows with the ad (approving the plan approves the picture), or that the plan's picture is gone; ads already running keep theirs, and a new one waits for a new version of the plan (`update_marketing_plan` makes one, with a new picture once Admiry has one). When there is an `image`, describe it to an owner who cannot see it in its `alt` words, and never invent what it shows. Never promise NewsBreak without a picture: a trade Admiry has no picture for yet gets NewsBreak only as a later line ("once Admiry has a picture for your trade"). And never promise a place the plan does not fund: Admiry funds NewsBreak only for a business that prepays, with a budget and a service that suit it.
5. Ask whether it looks right. To change it, `update_marketing_plan`: the owner's words in `message`, or `changes` when you know the field (the budget, the area, the offer). A yes changes nothing and needs no call: go on to step 6.
6. Before it can run, in this order: the sign-in, the page, how the ads run and, for ads Admiry runs from a prepaid balance, the payment. `get_marketing_state` lists them under `setup`, and says whether the steps are done and the launch's own check passes (`ready_to_launch`), with that check's refusal in `blocked` when it does not (an undone step shows in the steps themselves); its `next` asks the first one left:
   - **Sign-in.** If the owner has not signed in, `connect_account` returns their sign-in link.
   - **The page.** It comes first: before how the ads run and before any payment. Put the three ways to the owner side by side and let them choose (`next` offers them; never choose for them): Admiry builds it, you design it and Admiry hosts it, or the website they already have. Admiry builds it: `update_landing_page` with `action: "write"` has Admiry write it and answers with a private preview link and a `token`. Show the link. `revise` and `edit` change the page, and each answers with a new link and a new `token`: publish the latest one. Publishing takes the owner's yes, so the first `publish` call says what will change and the same call, with the same `token` and `owner_confirmed: true`, does it. For a home-services business or a dental practice, where Admiry's own sites are on, `write` starts a whole website instead of the one page: Admiry asks, one question at a time, the look first and then the facts it lacks (answer with `action: "build_site"`, `style` and `facts`), then shows the site with its photo checklist and the link where the owner adds their logo and photos; the same two-call yes publishes it once its logo (or the owner's business name as the logo, `wordmark: true`) and one real photo of the business are in. The owner's uploads rebuild the preview and end the earlier link: when they say the photos are in, read `get_landing_page` with `view: "site"` and publish with the `token` it shows now. You design it and Admiry hosts it: `action: "preview_html"` for one page you write, or `action: "upload_site"` for a whole site you build (read the kit's `bundle` section first), then `publish` with the owner's yes, in the same two calls. Every page carries Admiry's script tag exactly as `get_landing_page` with `view: "kit"` gives it (`tracking.snippet`): it measures the calls, forms and bookings the ads bring. An upload with a page that lacks it is refused with that fix; a `preview_html` page without it gets it added when it is published. Before you build, tell the owner in a sentence that Admiry hosts it on an address it gives the site, made from the business's name (like bright-smile-dental.business.admiry.ai), and say Admiry's own words on hosting: "Hosting is free while your ads with Admiry spend at least $200 a month. Without that, the site comes down 30 days later. We remind you first." The website they already have: `action: "use_url"` with its address. A website of the business's own can be the ads' page once Admiry's code is on it; until then the answer that built the plan offered the ways to put it there: the steps and the code (`get_landing_page` with `view: "kit"`), an e-mail of them to whoever runs the site (`action: "send_install_steps"`, their address as `email`), Admiry's team (`action: "request_install_help"`, with `contact` when the owner wants to be reached at another address than their sign-in one), or an Admiry page instead (`action: "write"`). Never say the code is on the site yourself: `get_landing_page` with `view: "check"` and the website as `candidate_url` says whether it is, and `action: "use_url"` then makes the website the ads' page, with the owner's yes. Admiry's page never states a price, a number of years, a rating or a licence number the business has not given it: to show one, add it with `update_business_profile` first, in `usp` or `pricing_hints` (each a list of sentences: send the whole list, with what is already on file; a note does not reach the page). For NewsBreak the page's footer also needs a Terms link and the business's phone or e-mail: Admiry's own page carries both once the phone is on file.
   - **Where the ads run.** Ask the owner how their ads should run and never choose for them: put both ways side by side, then call `connect_account` with their answer. For Google Search: Admiry runs the ads from a prepaid balance, with no Google account needed (`mode: "managed"` answers the link where the owner chooses that way, accepts the terms and adds funds); or the owner's own Google Ads account (their 10-digit Customer ID as `account_id`: Admiry sends an invitation they accept in Google Ads). Called with neither before the owner has chosen, `connect_account` changes nothing and answers the question itself (`status` `choose`, with both choices and the arguments each sends). A place Admiry runs from the owner's balance (NewsBreak) needs funds instead: `get_billing` with `view: "topup_link"`. Either may first return a link where the owner accepts the agreement. One place that is ready is enough: a place that is not ready waits and does not hold the others back, and each place's line under `setup` says what it still waits for (`reason`, for a place with nothing to connect; a place the owner connects says it in `text`) and what its page still needs (`page`). For a business that prepays, Google Search's line says what it still waits for: its funds, or Admiry getting its account ready.
   - **The payment.** For ads Admiry runs from a prepaid balance, the owner pays on the link Admiry gives: the first payment's link is `connect_account`'s for that way (`mode: "managed"`), and it opens the plan's own page, with its summary, the agreement and the prepayment terms, which the owner ticks themselves, and one button to pay on Stripe; `get_billing` with `view: "topup_link"` gives the link for adding funds later. Give the owner the link; never tick or pay for them.
7. `launch_marketing_plan`. The first call returns the owner's approval page, or says what is still missing. On the page the owner reads the plan and either approves it there or reads you the code it shows. With a code, call the tool again with `approval_code` exactly as they say it. Each place starts when it is ready (its account or its funds; for NewsBreak also its picture and its page's Terms link and phone or e-mail); `say` tells which are live and which wait, and for what. If no place can start yet, the launch is refused with what each place waits for, one sentence each, and the code is not used up; `setup` says the same beforehand. Asking to launch again returns the same approval page, and a code the owner already read stays good. If the owner approved on the page, there is nothing more to send: `get_campaigns` shows where each place stands. With Admiry's launch review on, a business's first launch may instead wait in Admiry's review once the owner says yes (`plan_status` `in_review`): Admiry checks the ads and sets them up with Google, and they go live within one business day. Asking to launch again, or sending a code then, is answered that it is in review; there is nothing to call meanwhile.

After the first plan, Admiry may research the business's market in the background; `get_research` shows it once it is ready, and the next rewrite of the plan's words uses it (a change of the budget, area, offer, objective or a channel's amount alone does not). The owner can ask for it again with `request_research` (`topics`, and their own question as `note`), at most once a day.
