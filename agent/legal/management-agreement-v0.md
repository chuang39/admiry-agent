# Admiry Google Ads Management Agreement

This agreement is between you, the business owner who accepts it on the Admiry connect page, and Admiry. It covers Admiry running ads for your business inside your own Google Ads account, which you link to Admiry.

## 1. Scope of authority

When you link your Google Ads account to Admiry, you allow Admiry to:

- create, change, pause and end ads for your business within the monthly limit you set;
- read how your ads perform and what Google charges you, to report it to you and to improve your results;
- carry out the plans you approve: nothing that spends money starts until you approve it with your approval code or on your plan page.

Admiry does not change your billing, your payment method, the people who can use your account, or who owns it. The account and everything in it stay yours.

## 2. Fees

$0 platform fee. No management fee during early access. Google charges you directly for your ads, and Admiry never marks those charges up.

Admiry may introduce a management fee later. If it does, it will tell you by e-mail at least 30 days before the fee applies, with the amount and how it is worked out. Before the fee applies you may end this agreement and unlink your account without any penalty or charge.

## 3. No guarantee of results

Estimates of customers, calls or costs are estimates. Admiry does not guarantee any number of customers, calls or sales, any other result, or any cost per result.

## 4. Your data

Admiry reads the data in your Google Ads account only to run, measure and report on your ads. It does not sell your data and does not use it to run ads for anyone else. Admiry keeps a record of every change it makes, which you or your assistant can read at any time.

## 5. Your responsibilities

You confirm that what you tell Admiry about your business is accurate, that you may authorise ads for it, and that you follow the Google Ads terms and policies that apply to your account.

## 6. Ending this agreement and unlinking

You can end this agreement at any time by removing Admiry from your Google Ads account (Admin, then Access and security) or by asking Admiry through your assistant. Admiry stops making changes as soon as the link ends. Admiry may end this agreement with notice by e-mail. Ending it does not undo charges Google already made.

## 7. Changes to this agreement

When this agreement changes, Admiry publishes the new version on this page and asks you to accept it before it links, changes or starts anything further.
