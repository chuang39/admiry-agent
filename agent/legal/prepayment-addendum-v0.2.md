# Admiry Prepayment Addendum

This addendum adds to the Google Ads Management Agreement. It applies when you choose, on the Admiry connect page, that Admiry runs your ads in a Google Ads account it opens for your business and that you pay for those ads in advance. Where the two differ for such a business, this addendum wins.

## 1. The account Admiry opens for you

Admiry opens a Google Ads account for your business, runs your ads in it and pays Google for them. Each business gets its own account; it is never shared with another advertiser. Admiry shows you the account's number and completes Google's advertiser verification for it on your behalf, so your ads name the right advertiser.

## 2. Your balance

You add funds by card or by bank transfer on Stripe's secure page; Admiry never sees your card or your bank login. Each payment is between the minimum and maximum shown on the add-funds page. Your balance is:

- spent only on this business's ads, at Google's exact cost: Admiry never marks Google's charges up, and your statement shows Google's exact charges;
- refundable: on request, Admiry refunds the unspent balance to the card or bank account that paid it;
- non-interest-bearing: it earns no interest.

## 3. When the balance runs out

When your balance reaches $0, Admiry pauses your ads until you add funds. Admiry never charges your card or debits your bank account by itself: no card or bank account is kept on file.

## 4. Fees

$0 platform fee. No management fee during early access. When you add funds, the price of each way of paying includes a transaction fee: what Stripe charges to process your card payment or bank transfer. Admiry adds nothing to it and passes it on at cost; the add-funds page shows the price and its transaction fee before you pay, and the rest of the price is added to your balance. The transaction fee is not refunded, because Stripe keeps it. Section 2 of the agreement applies to any fee Admiry introduces later.

## 5. Ending

You can end this addendum at any time by asking Admiry through your assistant. Admiry then stops your ads and refunds the unspent balance to the card or bank account that paid it. Ending it does not undo what Google already charged for ads that ran.
