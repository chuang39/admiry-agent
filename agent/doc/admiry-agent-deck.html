An assistant can plan and analyze. It cannot buy media, host a landing page, update a Google Business Profile, answer a tracked phone line or reconcile spend to bookings. That plumbing does not exist for the long tail.

The assistant is the interface. Admiry is the pipes.

Built for Meta Muse first. ChatGPT, Claude and any MCP-capable assistant use the same connector.

Tell. Approve. Run.

  1. Tell

    "I run a dental practice in San Jose. I want more Invisalign patients."

  2. Approve

    One plan: budget split, pages, tracking, an estimated lead range with its basis. The owner approves once, in the assistant or on one web page.

  3. Run and report

    Pages go live, tracking turns on, campaigns launch in the owner's own accounts. Every week, a summary in plain English.

Every job feeds the next.

Conversion feeds reviews and the platforms' own optimizers. Optimization feeds the next plan. The loop closes inside one agent; a stack of eight vendors never closes it.

Your business feeds business knowledge, which drives SEO and GEO, ads and the website. Those create discovery, traffic, conversion, leads and bookings, revenue and optimization, which feeds back into the plan.
