# Home services — plumbing

How a plumber's ads catch emergencies and bigger jobs, and which searches to keep out.

## Key points

- Split emergencies (a burst pipe, no hot water, a blocked drain) from projects (water heaters, repipes, sewer lines) (our reading) [research-verticals].
- Search themes: "emergency plumber <town>" (if the hours cover it), "water heater replacement", "drain cleaning near me", "sewer line repair".
- Keep out: plumbing jobs and apprenticeships, plumbing supply stores, how to unclog, plumbing codes.
- Plumbers are not on Google's Advanced Verification list [g-av]; Texas plumbing ads carry the responsible master plumber's license number [tx-plumb-ads], Illinois's the plumbing license number [il-plumb-ads].

## Details

A plumber's average job is small, so repeat customers and the bigger jobs carry the cost of a lead. The research document weighs a
$129.02 cost per lead against a $341 average job and concludes that repeat customers and larger jobs (water heaters, sewer lines)
must carry the economics; the Numbers give each figure's source and scope [research-verticals] [liq-home-2026] [ha-plumber].

An emergency (a burst pipe, no hot water, a blocked drain) is a call now: the ad shows the call button and, when the hours cover
it, the word "emergency". A project (a water heater, a repipe, a sewer line) is a quote or a visit. The research document lists a
24/7 emergency banner with tap-to-call, price ranges, financing and a maintenance-plan signup among a plumber's page modules
[research-verticals]. The federal tax credits for heat pump, gas and solar water heaters ended with 2025 [irs-25c] [irs-25d].

Google began moving Local Services campaigns into Google Ads in August 2026, and plumbing is among the trades named in the first
phase [research-verticals]; the Google Search file has the detail.

Advanced Verification names only garage door services (United States) and locksmith services (United States and Canada); its copy
rules on service-call fees, starting prices, coupons and rapid-response claims are written for them, and plumbers are not on its
list [g-av]. The compliance file has the rules. Keeping an arrival time out of an ad unless every call can be met is still our
advice.

Texas. The rule asks that every advertisement a plumbing company designs to solicit plumbing business state the license number of
its responsible master plumber of record, regardless of the type of media used. Its exemptions, some on condition of a statement
(that local license numbers are available on request or, for an ad with no visual component, that the company complies with state
licensing), are national or interstate television, a franchisor's internet ads for its franchisees, ads with no visual component,
items of nominal value, signs at the business and third-party listings the company neither edits nor promotes; none is a
plumber's own search ad, so our reading is that such an ad carries the number [tx-plumb-ads].

Illinois. The state health department says every advertisement for plumbing services includes the plumbing license number,
under the Plumbing License Law (225 ILCS 320/5) [il-plumb-ads]. The compliance file lists the other states.

## Numbers

- Plumbing's average cost per lead in search advertising: $129.02, from LocaliQ's 2025 home-services report (its customers' US search campaigns on Google Ads and Microsoft Ads: 3,211 in all, at least 103 in each category, April 2024 to March 2025; its "averages" are medians) [liq-home-2026].
- A plumbing job: $341 on average, in a normal range of $182–$501 (HomeAdvisor's cost guide, page updated June 2026; the page names no region) [ha-plumber].
- A water heater replacement: $1,348 on average, in a normal range of $882–$1,826 (HomeAdvisor's cost guide, page updated June 2026; the page names no region) [ha-water-heater].
- Average ticket (revenue per paying customer) from plumbing Local Services leads: $1,714 (SearchLight's benchmark, February 2026: 230 plumbing accounts in a sample of 888 home-services contractors) [searchlight-lsa].
- Local Services plumbing leads: $69 each (The Media Captain's average, compiled from data on more than 100 of its clients in an article dated August 2025 that gives no period); SearchLight's February 2026 benchmark has $57 a lead for plumbing [mediacaptain-lsa] [searchlight-lsa].

## Do not say

- A federal tax credit for a water heater (ended) [irs-25c] [irs-25d].
