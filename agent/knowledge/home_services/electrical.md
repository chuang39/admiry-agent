# Home services — electrical

How an electrician's ads catch repairs and upgrades, and which searches to keep out.

## Key points

- Emergencies and repairs want a call; EV chargers and standby generators want a quote request [research-verticals] (panel upgrades too, in our reading).
- Search themes: "electrician <town>", "panel upgrade", "EV charger installation", "outlet repair near me".
- Keep out: electrician jobs, apprenticeships and schools, electrical supply, wiring diagrams.
- Electricians are not on Google's Advanced Verification list [g-av].
- Texas electrical contracting companies put the contractor's name and license number in ads [tx-elec-ads]; Florida electrical contractors, the registration or certification number [fl-489-521]; New Jersey, the contractor's name, business permit number and address [nj-elec-ads].

## Details

The research document sets a $93.69 cost per lead against a $351 average job and lists panel and EV-charger upsells beside them; our
reading is that the upgrades carry the economics. The Numbers give each figure's source and scope [research-verticals]
[liq-home-2026] [ha-electrician].

A repair or an emergency is a call: the research document gives electricians the call to action "Call" and lists an emergency banner
and panel, EV and lighting pages among their page modules; it gives EV charger and standby generator installation "Request a
quote". A panel upgrade has no row there, so its quote request is our reading [research-verticals].

Utility rebate information is among an EV charger page's modules in the research document: an ad names a rebate only while the utility
offers it [research-verticals]. The federal home-charger credit covers property placed in service at a main home from 2023-01-01 to
2026-06-30, so none installed later (our reading) [irs-30c].

Advanced Verification names only garage door and locksmith services, and its copy rules (service-call fees, starting prices, coupons,
rapid-response claims) are written for them; electricians are not on its list [g-av]. The compliance file has the rules; keeping an
arrival time out of an ad unless every call can be met is our advice.

Texas. The rule asks that all advertising by electrical contracting companies designed to solicit electrical business include the
electrical contractor's name and license number. Its exemptions are nationally placed television (with a
statement that license numbers are available on request), telephone book listings of name, address and number only, manufacturers'
and distributors' telephone book trade ads, telephone solicitations (the caller says the contractor complies with state licensing
and gives the number on request), items of nominal value and signs at the business; none names a search ad, so our reading is that
such an ad carries the name and number [tx-elec-ads]. The compliance file lists the other states.

Florida. The statute asks that the contractor's registration or certification number be stated in each offer of services, business
proposal or advertisement the contractor uses, regardless of medium. Business stationery and promotional novelties are not
advertisements under it, and its exemption for newspaper, magazine, flyer, billboard, phone book, Internet and broadcast
advertisements is for alarm system contracting only [fl-489-521].

New Jersey. All advertising by a licensed electrical contractor displays its name, the words "Electrical Contractor Bus. Permit #"
with the number, and its street address [nj-elec-ads].

## Numbers

- Electricians & Electrical Contractors' average cost per lead in search advertising: $93.69 (LocaliQ's 2025 home-services report: its customers' US search campaigns on Google Ads and Microsoft Ads, 3,211 in all, at least 103 in each category, April 2024 to March 2025; its "averages" are medians) [liq-home-2026].
- An electrician job: $351 on average, in a normal range of $163–$538 (HomeAdvisor's cost guide, page updated June 2026; the page names no region) [ha-electrician].
- Average ticket (revenue per paying customer) from electrical Local Services leads: $1,434 (SearchLight's benchmark, February 2026: 112 electrical accounts in a sample of 888 home-services contractors; the same row has $39 a lead) [searchlight-lsa].
- "electrician near me" ran 67 on Google Trends against 100 for "plumber near me" (US, the 12 months to 2026-10-05; relative interest, not search counts, and the research document calls its Trends figures broad-match) [research-verticals].

## Do not say

- A federal tax credit for EV chargers or panel upgrades (both ended) [irs-30c] [irs-25c].
