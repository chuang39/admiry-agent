# Home services — roofing

How a roofer's ads catch repairs, replacements and storm damage, and which searches to keep out.

## Key points

- Replacements want a quote request [research-verticals]; leaks and repairs a call, and a free inspection only if the owner offers one (our reading).
- After a storm, name storm-damage repair and a written estimate; never invite an insurance claim or offer to file, handle, help with or negotiate one, cover or waive a deductible, or promise that insurance pays [fl-489-147] [tx-ins-4102-163] [tx-bc-27-02] [tdi-roofing].
- Search themes: "roof repair <town>", "roof replacement", "roof leak repair", "roofing contractor near me".
- Keep out: roofing jobs, shingles and materials for sale, do-it-yourself repair, roof racks.

## Details

Roofing has the highest cost per lead of the 16 home-services categories in LocaliQ's report; the research document reads a $9,609
average replacement as still leaving it among the stronger ratios of ticket to lead cost [liq-home-2026] [ha-roof]
[research-verticals].

A replacement wants a quote request, the call to action the research document gives roofers, and a leak a call (our reading); it also
finds that storm-driven demand suits a fast launch [research-verticals].

Name a manufacturer certification only when the owner names it.

Insurance claims. A roofer's ad stays off claim handling and deductible offers:
- Florida, Fla. Stat. 489.147: a contractor may not interpret policy provisions, advise an insured on coverage or duties, or adjust
  a claim for the insured unless it holds a public adjuster license, (2)(d); may not offer a residential property owner a rebate,
  gift, coupon, deductible waiver or other thing of value in exchange for allowing a roof inspection or making a roof claim, (2)(b);
  and may not solicit with a communication that encourages a consumer to contact a contractor or public adjuster to make a
  roof-damage claim unless it states, in type at least 12 points and at least half as large as the largest, that the owner pays any
  deductible, that knowingly or willfully paying, waiving or rebating any part of one with intent to injure, defraud or deceive is
  a third-degree felony, and that intentionally filing a false, incomplete or misleading claim is one too, (1)(a) and (2)(a). Soliciting means contact with a specific person,
  (1)(c). Up to $10,000 a violation, (3) [fl-489-147].
- Texas: a contractor may not act as a public adjuster or advertise to adjust claims on any property it is providing or may provide
  contracting services for, whether or not it holds a public adjuster license or a power of attorney (Ins. Code 4102.163(a))
  [tx-ins-4102-163]. Advertising or promising a good or service paid from a claim while, without the insurer's consent, paying,
  waiving, absorbing or rebating the deductible, or otherwise helping the insured avoid it, is a Class B misdemeanor (Bus. & Com.
  Code 27.02(c)(1) and (d)) [tx-bc-27-02]. The Department of Insurance lists as improper offering to negotiate a claim settlement or
  file a claim for the policyholder, and promising to recover "every dime you are owed" [tdi-roofing].

Our reading: other states may have similar rules, so a search ad names repair and an estimate, not claim help.

## Numbers

- Roofing & Gutters' average cost per lead in search advertising: $228.15, the highest of the report's 16 home-services categories (LocaliQ's 2025 home-services report: its customers' US search campaigns on Google Ads and Microsoft Ads, 3,211 in all, at least 103 in each category, April 2024 to March 2025; its "averages" are medians) [liq-home-2026].
- A roof replacement: $9,609 on average, which the page calls the national average, with most homeowners spending $5,902–$13,377 (HomeAdvisor's cost guide, page updated June 2026) [ha-roof].
- Local Services roofing leads: $162 each (The Media Captain's average, compiled from data on more than 100 of its clients in an article dated August 2025 that gives no period) [mediacaptain-lsa].

## Do not say

- "Your insurance will pay for a new roof."
- "Free roof."
- "We cover, waive or rebate your deductible."
- "We file or handle your insurance claim."
