# Home services — Google Search

How homeowners search for a trade, which searches to buy and which to keep out.

## Key points

- Two kinds of search: an emergency (call now) and a project (a quote or a visit); give each its own ads (our reading) [research-verticals].
- Search themes: the job with the town or "near me" ("water heater replacement <town>"); "emergency" only when the hours cover it.
- Keep out: jobs, salaries, apprenticeships, licensing classes; how-to and do-it-yourself; parts, supplies, big-box stores; manuals; other trades.
- Homeowners call: the ad's call button and the page carry the same phone.
- Google began moving Local Services for select home-service advertisers into Google Ads in August 2026 [lsa-pmax].

## Details

Homeowners search the job and the place, such as "water heater replacement <town>" or "plumber near me", and each kind of search
needs its own ad. Someone with a burst pipe wants to call now, so an emergency ad shows the call button and the hours. Someone
planning a new roof, a remodel or a replacement system wants a quote or a visit [research-verticals].

The "keep out" searches are people looking for work or training, a do-it-yourself fix, parts or supplies, a manual, or a different
trade. None of them is looking to hire the business, so they belong on the negative list.

Google Local Services lists, among others, these home trades in the US: plumbers, HVAC, electricians, roofers, landscaping, lawn
care, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, general contractors, handymen, tree services, pest control, locksmiths and garage door
services [lsa-us]. In August 2026 Google began moving the Local Services campaigns of select home and storefront service
advertisers into Google Ads as Performance Max campaigns with pay-per-lead goals; the first phase names plumbing, HVAC,
electrical, appliance repair, house cleaning, lawn care, roofing, pest control and moving. New pay-per-lead campaigns still start
in Local Services, not Google Ads, and the categories not moved in 2026 are due to move in 2027 [lsa-pmax]. What a Local Services
lead costs and books is in the Numbers, from one vendor's benchmark [searchlight-lsa].

A new Google Ads account may serve few ads at first: Google limits the impressions of an advertiser it does not yet judge
qualified, and account maturity is one factor it weighs; our reading is that a new account is exposed [g-limited].

Each trade's seasons are in its own file.

## Numbers

- "Home & Home Improvement" average cost per lead in search advertising: $90.92, from LocaliQ's 2026 benchmarks (its customers' Google Ads and Microsoft Ads campaigns; the page does not say mean or median); LocaliQ's home-services report gives the same $90.92 as the 2025 average for home services (3,211 US search campaigns, April 2024 to March 2025; its "averages" are medians) [liq-search-2026] [liq-home-2026].
- "Google sees almost 180,000 searches for 'plumber near me'" every month in the US, in LocaliQ's words; the page names no source or date for the figure [liq-home-2026].
- Local Services across 888 home-services contractors (SearchLight's benchmark: 1,774 campaigns, February 2026): $53 a lead; 43.9 % of leads booked an appointment; $233 of spend per paying customer [searchlight-lsa].
- Average ticket (revenue per paying customer) from those Local Services leads: $1,826 [searchlight-lsa].

## Do not say

- Another company's name.
- "Same day" or "emergency" unless the hours say so.
- "Licensed" for a registration, or without the number where the state asks.
